\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\noindent\textbf{MLLM agents for embodied tasks.}
EmbodiedBench~\cite{yang2025embodiedbench} evaluates multimodal LLMs on high-level and low-level embodied tasks
through a standardized action interface; Embodied Agent Interface~\cite{li2024embodied} and
NavBench~\cite{qiao2025navbench} probe embodied decision making and navigation reasoning. One line of work
improves such agents by embodied pre-training, online reinforcement learning, or policy-level
reasoning~\cite{chen2025transforming,wang2025vagen,zhang2025embodiedx,liu2025robogpt,anon2026policy}; another
improves them at test time with reflection, experience, memory, or
tools~\cite{shinn2023reflexion,zhao2023expel,chen2024automanual,wang2024agent,lei2025robomemory,ma2026brainmem,lee2026emho,zhou2026enabling,singhi2026think}.
On EB-Navigation, the strongest reported results draw the ground-truth target box into a simulator-rendered
bird's-eye view, optionally with reinforcement fine-tuning~\cite{wang2025seenav}, or let an 8B or larger model
call a library of perception and mapping tools~\cite{zhou2026enabling}. These methods keep the model in charge of
the plan. \ours instead removes planning from the model: the VLM is a semantic sensor, and state estimation and
action selection are explicit.

\noindent\textbf{Zero-shot object navigation with foundation models.}
Modular zero-shot ObjectNav combines open-vocabulary perception, a map of explored space, and a planner.
CoW~\cite{gadre2022cows} pairs open-vocabulary localization with frontier exploration; later systems build
vision-language value maps~\cite{yokoyama2023vlfm}, online scene graphs reasoned over by an
LLM~\cite{yin2024online,huang2025msgnav}, universal goal graphs~\cite{yin2025unigoal}, world
models~\cite{nie2025wmnav}, cognitive state machines~\cite{cao2024cognav}, or structured representations queried
by a VLM~\cite{zhu2025strive}. Instruction-following systems similarly ground language in maps and scene
graphs~\cite{long2024instructnav,rana2023sayplan,werby2024hierarchical}. Closest to our memory, ApexNav fuses
target-centric detections over time~\cite{zhang2025apexnav}, and AECNav consolidates detections into beliefs that
treat expected but absent detections as negative evidence~\cite{liu2026aecnav}. \ours shares the view that the
map must keep evidence outside the current image, but uses a small generative VLM as its only perception model
and keeps a short list of metric target hypotheses instead of a semantic map or scene graph.

\noindent\textbf{3D semantic representations and memory.}
Open-vocabulary 3D scene graphs~\cite{gu2023conceptgraphs,werby2024hierarchicalx}, online semantic
maps~\cite{jiang2025dualmap}, and language-embedded radiance or Gaussian
fields~\cite{qin2023langsplat,katragadda2025online,lee2026embodiedsplat} attach semantics to geometry, and
embodied memory systems keep spatial and temporal context available after it leaves the
image~\cite{yang2024scene,anwar2024remembr,ginting2025enter}. \ours sits at the minimal end of this spectrum: a
grounded box is lifted with depth and pose to one metric point, and nearby points are fused. This keeps the
memory small enough for a single consumer GPU shared with the simulator.

\noindent\textbf{Grounding, verification, and zoom.}
Set-of-Mark prompting~\cite{yang2023mark} and PIVOT~\cite{nasiriany2024pivot} make VLM decisions spatially
explicit, and navigation can be cast as visual question answering over depth-derived
options~\cite{goetting2024navigation}. Verification and test-time computation improve embodied
decisions~\cite{kwok2025robomonkey,dao2026direct,choi2026scale}, and calibrated uncertainty lets robots defer or
keep exploring~\cite{ren2023robots,liang2024introspective,ren2024explore}. \ours re-checks an uncertain box on a
magnified crop and tiles the frame when nothing is found, and spends these extra queries only when the evidence
is weak.

\noindent\textbf{Geometry from images.}
Learned reconstruction and depth priors recover geometry from
RGB~\cite{wang2025vggt,murai2024mast,maggio2025vggt,wen2025foundationstereo,yang2024depthv2,lin2025depth}, and
the choice of sensors can change the conclusions of embodied
evaluations~\cite{corlito2026sensor,wang2025rethinking,cai2025navdp}. We therefore report \ours both with a depth
sensor and with a monocular depth estimate.

\noindent\textbf{Concurrent work.}
Li et al.~\cite{li2026structure} also run Qwen3-VL-4B zero-shot and treat its answers as evidence rather than
actions, accumulating per-direction scores in a bird's-eye semantic field for vision-language navigation. \ours
targets object navigation, lifts VLM boxes to metric 3D hypotheses, and is evaluated on the full official splits
of two object-navigation benchmarks.
