\caption{One step of \ours. The frozen VLM $f$ gives the presence $\pi_t$ and box $b_t$ of the phrase $s$ on the frame $I_t$; a weak answer is checked on a crop or on tiles, which sets its weight $a_t$. The box is lifted to a floor point $q_t$ with depth $D_t$ and pose $p_t$ and fused into a hypothesis $\mu_k$ within $r$, or starts one; $\gamma_t<1$ discounts a repeated view, and a hypothesis that should be seen but is not loses $\lambda$ of its evidence $w_k$. Above $\tau_c$ a hypothesis is committed, and the agent goes to $\mu_{k^\star}$; A* on the map $M_t$ gives the action $u_t$.}
